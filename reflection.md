# AI Use Reflection
I used ChatGPT throughout this assessment for both technical assistance and drafting support. Technically, it helped me understand GitHub, create the required Markdown files, structure the repository and write meaningful commit messages. It also helped me organise the proposal into the required sections and format the policy-options table.

For research and argument development, I used ChatGPT to identify relevant Australian Government and academic sources, explain how they related to course concepts such as task substitution, complementarity, displacement and labour-market adjustment, and help develop three policy options. I checked the sources and used them to develop the final argument rather than relying only on generated text.

One output I accepted and developed was the Employer AI Redeployment Partnership because it aligned with my chosen focus on productivity and worker redeployment. I also rejected a later suggestion to add another paragraph on freelance earnings because I felt the proposal already contained sufficient analysis for the final draft.

A key limitation was the risk of over-relying on AI-generated summaries or citations. This required checking source details and distinguishing illustrative policy assumptions from existing government policy.
